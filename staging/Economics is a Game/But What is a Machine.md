> [!DEF] Machine

The notion of a machine is inextricably linked to two other notions - one is Work and associated ideas like job, career, calling etc and the other is labor - which for the time we will limit to physical which includes toil and mental which will encompass not just knowledge work but also emotional labor. Of course emotional toil is a real thing and we can't paper it over, but everything comes at its correct place.
