One of the main schisms in modern thought can be summed up in a neat phrase. Do you have faith in the evidence of your eyes or do you have believe in the unseen?

Islam takes the second position uncompromisingly, in a way that can make even seasoned Horsemen of Apocalypse into nervous jitterbugs. The position can be best summed up as what Russell said - I will never die for my beliefs because I might be wrong. Even the blase Pascal's wager has more oomph than this - and it sounds disrespectful to call it a position - a hedge. A compulsive need to be on the right side of history will only ensure that you are eventually on the wrong side of it. Brng us to the dreaded word - compromise.

However Islam - literally willingful submisson to the Will of Allah is unflinching in compromise. There are no half measures, no escae haches. The Will of Allah is absolute. Where is the compromise? The compromise is in the believer. The compromise is believing in the unseen. To be steadfast. To know that there is One power that knows better. They plan and Allah plans, is not said in jest. No matter how absolue a science and how powerful a tyrant. There is an OverTyrant over them all, the only one who can have arrogance. He is the only one on whom pride suits. He knows not just the unknown but the unknownable. The things that have no words - he just says Qun and it is. That is what modern thought is up against.
