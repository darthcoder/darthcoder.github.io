
Graduating at the bottom of his class, but graduating beyond the shadow of all doubt magikcs - Grug the Ork could feel a sense of satisfaction at not having strayed too far from his roots. He graduated the big brain humie class as a Shadow Hedge Wyzard - imagine a stuntie assassin and you would understand his consternation. The Night Elf lass - the Soul Cypher graduating from the top with gold medals in all kinds of magic and choosing the most arcane touchy feely magic. Grug could almost feel sorry for her, were he genetically capable of feeling. But this was the secret that Grug hid from everyone - especially his own self. He felt everything all the time. And given he was an ork and destined for guard duty at some overlords dungeon - but the degree meant going out in the world and adventuring mixing potions - truth be told there was more to grug than 3 potions and 2 debuffs. He had read a lot of library books but they made his head hurt simply by reading their names. He almost called a Waagh involuntarily when he saw a book on Banach Blazes, they were giving random names to elements now, this was almost too much.

  

“Do you never just shut up?” Grug almost dropped his graduation cap in surprise. Dont worry, came the honeyed reply, but thanks to me, all your secrets are safe with us. See, the Soul Psyker continued, bloodlines, what a weird thing huh. Not only did they make you an eyesore but they gave me such powers at birth that no one of them will master in an eternity. For what they stumble through, i need only demand. 

  

And that makes you interesting? Grug wanted to say, but you know our monosyllabic brute remained silent. Not good. 

  

What makes me interesting, the Soul Psyker tittered. What makes anyone interesting is not what they are but what they can be. No, not quite, it’s the journey that taketh the kith, as his grandma used to feel, not the kith that taketh the journey. Yes she agreed, what if someone refused? What if they just didn’t go on the journey they were supposed to go to? Im planning on going in hiding, making you the key and then taking you with me. It’s gonna be perfect. And I get to have an authentic adventure, without getting meddled over by my mother. 

  

Grug stared at her, she was still intoning from the lectern the perfect words of the perfect little valedictorian. 

  

Has it always been like this. Hedge Wizards are dime a dozen and he had always wanted to be as nondescript as possible. He couldn’t be a light hedge wizard if he wanted to be as it didn’t go with his genetic makeup. A heavy hedge wizard is just a fat kith. Shadow hedge wizard - that sounded deliciously derelict enough to try.

  

Oh i have already done the spell, don’t worry all your hard works been your own, now I just bear witness to it. She replied with a nauseating sweet wink. Grug almost Waaaghed, a little waagh. Almost.
