## What is a Chantry

The word Chantry comes from [Dragon Age:Origins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Age%3A_Origins?useskin=vector) and its many floundering successors. A Chantry is basically just the Catholic Church but I use it to refer to Institutions in general and religious institutions are usually more commonly cited but I mean more specifically "sprawling bureaucrazy institutions of knowledge work" the 10th Level of DnD Hell.
