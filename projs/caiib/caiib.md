## Prep Workflow

### Phase 1: Notes (By Thursday)
- Finish comprehensive notes for all 4 papers
- Structure: Key terms, definitions, formulas (minimal)
- Goal: Have a reference to quiz yourself from

### Phase 2: Triage (Friday onwards)
- Review notes. Flag units by difficulty (Easy / Medium / Hard)
- Easy units: 5–10 practice Qs, move on
- Medium units: 10–15 practice Qs, note patterns
- Hard units: Skip or skim; rely on C-answers

### Phase 3: Drilling (2–3 weeks before each exam)
- Daily: 20–30 min of practice questions (Easy → Medium)
- Track accuracy by unit
- Focus on weak areas, but don't chase perfection

### Phase 4: Exam (Travel 40–60 km)
- Book exam dates now (don't defer)
- Note: Long travel day. Leave early. Arrive rested.
