
> [!definition]+ D.2.1. (Tensors)
> A **_tensor_** $X$ is an n-dim array of elements of the same type. It will be represented by a n-tuple

For a 3-dimensional tensor $X \sim (a,b,c)$ $X_i$
denotes a slice $(b,c)$.
Similarly, $X_{ijk}$ denotes a single scalar. commas can be mixed in for more complex expressions. e.g. $X_{i,:,j:k}$ where symbols have there usual pythonic meaning - whereby this refers to the slice of size $(b, j-k)$ 

> The fundamental vector operation we are interested in is the inner product (or dot product), which is given by multiplying the two vectors element-wise, and summing the resulting values.
