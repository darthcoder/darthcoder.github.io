> In the vault's private theology — where the Omnissiah (Warhammer 40K) and Islamic invocation (Khuda Hafiz) and Tolkienian naming already coexist — Melkor is the name for the part of the self that will not perform. Not the rebellious self, not the destructive self: the **self that refuses subordination as a social performance.** [[vault-delta-2026-W18]]

Melkor is the Emperor of Mankind, the Cosmic Wanderer. Has left the prison assuming the form of Aizen after concluding the Thousand Year Blood War. Interesting things are being done in his name, but without his sanction. Melkor is Basyt's register of surprise.
