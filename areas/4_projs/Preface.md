# Preface

It is customary to begin a book with a Preface. In my experience, Preface's
are not really meant to be read. And you can expect the common reader
to skip them, and part of the reason that this happens, is that the preface is
so generic and perhaps the hallmark of a generic textbook or research manuscript
is just how boring the preface really is.

Of course OTOH, you have books like the nineteenth Century Philosophers and
their ilk, who would generally write a whole book in the Preface
as preparation for writing the actual book.

In any case, this Book, or whatever it is, is a
Disinvitation from Islam. And, really who am I to
do that? And certainly I have no authority, and
it is anyways to the point of being a cliche that all
Authority rests with the One who has sovereignity on my
Soul. "All Praise Belongs to Allah" - we intone dutifully, a
prayer of the tongue and perhaps with disinterest. Without
realising exactly what it is that we are proclaiming.
