## 1. `areas/QuranNotesForSelf/Prologue.md` — NEW (May 16, 11:14)

Opening: *"A specter looms ominously over the free world. The specter of submission."* The Communist Manifesto inverted — "submission" replaces "communism." Then a compressed narrative of empire-decline-and-postwar-Allied-righteousness, deliberately delivered in the canon's own voice, before the turn: *"But the peace was a lie. The middle pivots to Tolkien: *"Melkor stirs, at last. It is now time to act. It was time to ask his loyal companions — yes of the Valar and of the Maiar, first to respond to the summons. In the Empire of shadows, the dark mark stirs."* Melkor "has the three silmarils and he comes" — "for the very throat of the empire that dares claim 'All lives matter, but some lives matter more than others.'" Souls as Melkor's currency.

Then the named adversaries: *"What, you Palantir watchers and Anduril fuckers expect the 'Dark Enemy of the World' to play by your puny rules!"* — Palantir Technologies and Anduril Industries (the Founders Fund / Thiel-orbit defense-tech firms named after Tolkien artifacts) called out by name, the Tolkien-naming-conventions of the AI/defense complex turned back against them. Followed by Iain Banks: *"You seek to overwhelm the board while Gurgeh is still designing the game. The Player of the Game has permission from Allah to cheat."*

The operational statement: *"the game is between Capital and labor. Do you think dark factories help your cause? Do you think money will have value if you had all of it? ... I have not made a single public move yet and you have raised the price of oil by three and asked people not to buy gold for a year. The market is pricing that in. I have already seen the future."*

The vault-level signature: *"The board changes, the rules mutate, the git history gets written from scratch."*

Closing: *"You plan and Allah plans. Lets establish who is the best of planners and who are merely pests in Eden... I will just be witnessing. Await my first move. React as hell."*

The folder now has 9 notes. The "Prologue" filename is significant — it stages the QuranNotesForSelf folder itself as a book whose preface is being written *after* the body has been drafted. The author is retroactively framing his Quran engagement as the opening to something larger.

When the action is structural (folder topology, retrieval), the weekly note empties.

Reading: **prose in this vault tracks tension, not productivity.** A silent weekly note this period is consistent with high productivity in folder space. The HRMS shelving on May 14 removed a tension; the silence since is a *post-resolution silence*, not a depressive one. Monday's RO visit will either reintroduce tension (and the weekly note will swell) or confirm the resolution (and the W21 weekly note may stay quiet).

Prologue is the first authored note to reference the vault's own substrate — *"the git history gets written from scratch"* is a line that only makes sense if you know your work lives in a git-tracked vault.
