We define the complexity of a behavior as equal to the
length of its description.

The length of a description of
a particular system’s behavior depends on the number
of possible behaviors that system could exhibit.

A system’s complexity profile is a
plot of the system’s complexity as a function of scale

[[Limits to production]]

A corollary of the trade-off between complexity and
scale is the trade-off between adaptability and efficiency.

A determination of when complexity is desirable is provided by the **Law of Requisite Variety** To be effective, a system must be at least as complex as the environmental behaviors to which it must differentially react.
