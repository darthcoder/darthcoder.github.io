
## Introduction

1. The entire universe belongs to Allah. Q. What does it mean to Worship?
2. There is another life after this one and we have to give an account of all our actions.
3. Whatever the Prophet presented was not from himself but from Allah as a Messenger.
## Verse 1-5
1. What has been revealed is the truth though most people don't believe it
2. The Sun and the moon and the Heavens are all subject to His Commandments and Laws. He arranges each matter. He details the signs so that you may be certain of meeting with your Lord.
	1. Allah's power holds everything in place. Nothing happens without His permission.
3. The regularity of matters on Earth, the harmony of seasons and the rhythm of the day and night, these are all signs of Allah for people who give thought.
4. In the same land and nourished with the same water, some trees bear more fruit than others, even though they are the same in nature and nurture. How can this difference be? Indeed this is a sign from Allah. 
5. People, especially those who sin, justify it by thinking that they will be dust and hence will escape the repercussions of their transgressions. However, Allah promises that He will bring them back and make them the Companions of the Fire.

## Verse 6-10

6. When people were brought the message of Allah by a Messenger, they would taunt the messengers and ask them to bring the scourge of Allah on them. This verse is to remind that not only is Allah full of  forgiveness to people despite their wrongdoings, but also is severe in penalty.
7. People also asked the Prophet to show them a sign. Here Allah clarifies that Huzur is just a messenger and a guide and a warner
	1. From the video of Nouman Ali Khan, once Allah has shown His sign to a people. they don't have the option to say no. Thus no signs were shown.
8. Again clarifying that Allah knows even the unseen.  [[#Verse 1-5]] point no 4. While we don't know what a woman carries in Her womb, Allah has full knowledge and knows if the child would bear fruit and hence be a good person or will die prematurely or whatever is in its heart.
9. Again mentioning that there are no things that happen without His permission. He knows both the unseen and the witnessed.
10. It matters not to Him whether we try to conceal or reveal something. He has full knowledge of all matters. He is in a one-on-one correspondence with the totality of the Universe.

## Verse 11-15

11. For every person there are successive Angels before and behind him who protect him by the decree of Allah. **The condition of a people will not be changed until they change themselves.** There is no patron besides Allah and once Allah decides ill for a people, there is no repelling it.
	1. Doubt: Can Allah change His mind?
12. The fear and anxiety that we feel about the future are due to Him. The thunder and lightning that causes us fear and aspiration are caused by Him.
13. He sends thunderbolts where He wills, while they dispute about Allah - Allah causes the universe to function as per His desires and command while the people of the World debate if He exists or not. And He is severe in assault. 
14. He is alone worthy of supplication. The example cited is of a person dying of thirst but too far to reach the body of water to quench his thirst and save himself. This is the fate of the supplication of the disbelievers.
	1. Who is a disbeliever?
15. The futility of disbelief is expressed. Everything in Creation submits to Allah. Prostrating to Allah is an act by a believer, but it is a willing expression of faith. By _sajdah_ I affirm that I submit to Allah.  Were I a non-believer and I didn't prostrate to Allah, whether I worshipped a different god, or were I an atheist or whether I worshipped my desires, it matters not, nothing happens to me that is without His permission.
