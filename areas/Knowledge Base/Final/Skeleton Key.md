# Uncertain  Iterated Prisoners Dilemma

To what extent are the strategies dependent on the payoffs? How do the strategies vary if the payoffs were variable and changed, i.e. can we quantify the dependence of the strategies on the payoffs? What if the payoffs were fixed for the whole sequence of games vs what if they varied in every iteration? What if they were chosen at random? What if you didn't know the exact payoffs but only knew them probabilistically?
