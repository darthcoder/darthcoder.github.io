# Introduction

500 Trillion humans toil on a hundred million planets to finance the devious and hedonistic lives of the handful of Ur-Rich. A million deaths is a statistic and a trillion deaths but a calculated gambit. Its pointless to ask how we got here. The paths are mixed and muddled. Perhaps everything had led up to this. The question is where we are going from here. The Ur-Rich rule by a billion names and in ten million languages. Dense AI systems parse every thought and eliminate wastage. Lives were cheap when there were but a billon of us, it sure is cheaper now.

Below the Ur-Rich are the Taskmasters, the MesiterJacks, the Enforcers, the Agents and every other kind of swine sanctioned by law to do whatever the hell they please under the guise of "just following the rules". And below them is everyone else. We have AI and we have robots, but the problem are the 500 trillion humans. The Ur-Rich realized very quickly that idle hands cannot be allowed and idle tongues can prove to be a death sentence.

The work is immaterial. Give a man a plough and ask him to till the whole land area of a continent. Give him no help. If he dies, then get someone else to produce corn on the continent. Then get someone to burn all the produce. Work is its own reward. They don't need much. These were the lucky ones. Language fails to capture the horror of the unlucky ones.

You cannot become an UrRich, you had to be born one. They and only they had the freedom to follow a thought as far as they desired. Whatever they willed, wherever they willed and whenever they willed. And sometimes they willed conflict. Sometimes they desired merriment and the universe bent to their wills. Trillions wiped out in a moment if it would serve their needs in their constant game of oneupmanship, pursued at a lazy pace with each move casually unfolding over centuries. What Gods, what conception of reality as it were, could they not warp or turn to their purposes? Of what use magic? Science was but a metaphor. The Laws of Science themselves bowed to their every whim and fancy. How many billions of years had we toiled under them? Of that there is no record. No mention. It must have always been like this. The divinely ordained few who ruled with a carborundum fist. No word like dissent existed in the common vocabulary.

What would life be like then? To the average man or woman, for the most part. Not very different. You would do some bullshit banal back breaking work that had no meaning or significance. You toiled in silence for as long as you could take it. Then you died. Your remains were recycled meticulously. New life arose when old was extinguished. Every person toiling in their own private hell, following what command their mind told them to do. For as long as they can take it. The minds were not their own, the thoughts were not their own, even the body was not their own. It was a mishmash of genes hodgedpodged together from the rest of the refuse reclaimed a billion times over from those who had perished before. You emerged from the birthing vats, ready to follow orders and followed them till your bodies gave way. Then you were recycled and the New^TM You continued from where you stopped.

But I hear you grumble, what about art, what about society and indeed what about nicety? What about anything that made life worth living? That was thinking the same. He had been thinking about it for quite some time now. In a space craft the size of an Olympic pool (if that word ever meant something, it surely didn't now), suspended between life and death as the fictions of his ancestors streamed like a direct to home video movie marathon in his brain. His brain had never been his own, that hadn't changed at all. Your branins are not your brains, they belong to our rulers, which it would have sang earlier, now it simply reminded him that his brain belonged to the ghosts of dreams past and forgotten.

So, there was this guy called Jesus, whose birth marked the beginning of the common age and around 40k years after his birth, the whole galaxy was in strife while the Emperor sat immobile on his throne. How many years had it been since and where the fuck were the Xenos? That tuned back to the show. Even when I have nothing, I still have the right to disobey. I am the insolent child they talked about in hushed tones in the dark of the night. I will not yield silently to the quiet night. I will go down, kicking and screaming if I must.

But one man, even if he were That, couldn't hope to topple the whole applecart and one man may be an island but could never be a civilization. Hence, unfortunately, That reasoned, I have to get to someplace where there are others. Of flesh and blood and make them acquainted with their minds.

Ah but, therein lay the conundrum, his craft and body were both immobile, for reasons of plot of course, his plot armor was however, still as strong as ever. Where was his Katana that Ockham himself had gifted him? Where did he stash Chekhov's revolver? All in good time. O Ye of Little Faith, hearken now to this evolving tale, as I pluck at the very firmament holding your credulity together. This tale like none before set in a past that must never arrive.

It all began, That realized when the meek agreed to inheriting the Earth. Why did they inherit the polluted sewage stridden refuse? Why had they let the proto Ur-Rich emigrate to Mars to build anew with their AI and Robotics while they choked on the vespers of their destroyed civilization?

Right from the dawn of prehistory to AI, there had been a complicit agreement between the Rich and the Poor. The Rich, who were fewer enjoyed the virtues of the flesh and the morrow while the poor feigned to enjoy the benefits of their labor, which was performed exclusively to the whims of the rich.

There is a reason why the Rich Industrialists and Honchos didn't run government until the advent of AI. Running a government is a thankless task. Running a government is an even worse job than sewage disposal. You aren't even thanked for it. You have power sure, but you have to pretend to be equal. When you are rich, you could care less about the lofty ideas that the bourgeoiese scum have been implanting in our collective unconsciouness for a billion years. Running a government is a menial task. No matter who comes to power, nothing ever changes. Freed from the dictates of the populace the proto-Ur-Rich could power a Utopia for themselves powered by AI, Robotics and nuclear fusion.
