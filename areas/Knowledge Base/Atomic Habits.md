# Make it obvious/invisible

Environment design. Restructure the environment so that good habits are obvious and incentivised. Make the good choice obvious where it is easy to pick up.

# Make it attractive/unattractive

The behaviors that are motivating to us are dependent upon the people who we are around. We are members of different tribes and their shared expectations are attractive. So joining a group that expects the kind of behavior you want to replicate will help in making it attractive.

Changing habits can mean changing tribes. We would be wrong with the crowd rather than correct alone. 

# Make it easy/difficult

Many of our behaviors are just about convenience. Won't check phone if it is kept a bit farther away. It is not just about increasing the pressure in the pipe, but it can often be about undoing the bend in the pipe to raise the flow rate. i.e. try to make it easier to do the thing you want to do.

## **What is the difference between a habit and an addiction?** 

A behavior that you continue to repeat despite bad outcomes. It is beyond habit. The feedback cue is broken. Or that it doesn't matter. You don't care about the feedback.
