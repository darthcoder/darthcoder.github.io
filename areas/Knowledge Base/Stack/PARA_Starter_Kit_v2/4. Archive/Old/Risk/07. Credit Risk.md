Oldest of the risk disciplines. Now because of derivatives, its closer to market risk.
Cost of replacing cash flows if counterparty (borrower) defaults.

Still requires construction of **Default Probabilities**, **Loss given default** and **Credit Exposures**

## Pre-Settlement risk

Risk that a counterparty has during the life of a transaction.

## Settlement risk
5 kinds:

1. Revocable - can cancel the transaction without counterparty consent.
2. Irrevocable - after payment is sent but before payment from other party is due.
3. Uncertain - After payment is due but before it is received.
4. Settled - After the counterparty payment is received.
5. Failed - After it is established that counterparty has not made the payment.

### Reduction

1. RTGS
2. Netting agreements
3. Netting agreements can be bilateral or multilateral
