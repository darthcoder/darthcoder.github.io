A daily planner app that integrates with your google calendar. You input a prompt like: Block a meeting from 4 to 5 with Michael and it will create a meeting for that period in your calendar. You can also query your calendar in natural language.

This will be an app in Android as well as iPhone. Developed in React Native as JavaScript(NodeJS) has official bindings for ChatGPT from OpenAI and I want to use those.
