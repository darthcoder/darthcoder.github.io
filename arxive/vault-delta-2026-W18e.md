## WHAT THIS MEANS

The vault is returning to its core tension: the machinery of belief-formation.

1. **The body-institution intersection** — how faith bodies (religious institutions) intersect with secular reasoning
2. **The Omnissiah-Islam unification** — the machine vs. the unseen
3. **What the vault does not write** — the vault has been tracking what it *avoids* writing; this note names that avoidance directly

"Faith Vs Belief" is not speculation. It is naming the schism that has sat beneath the vault's architecture since its founding. The vault writes about machines, about systems, about structures—but stops short of the final question: *what do you actually believe?*

This draft is the vault asking itself that question directly.
