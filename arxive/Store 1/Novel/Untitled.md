500 Trillion humans toil on a hundred million planets to finance the devious and hedonistic lives of the handful of Ur-Rich. A million deaths is a statistic and a trillion deaths but a calculated gambit. Its pointless to ask how we got here. The paths are mixed and muddled. Perhaps everything had led up to this. The question is where we are going from here. The Ur-Rich rule by a billion names and in a million languages. Dense AI systems parse every thought and eliminate wastage. Lives were cheap when there were but a billon of us, it sure is cheaper now. 

Below the Ur-Rich are the Taskmasters. And below them is everyone else. We have AI and we have robots, but the problem are the 500 trillion humans. The Ur-Rich realized very quickly that idle hands lead to revolutions. Thus only the Taskmasters and above are allowed to use labor saving devices. The work is immaterial. Give a man a plough and ask him to till the whole land area of a continent. Give him no help. If he dies, then get someone else to produce corn on the continent. Then get someone to burn all the produce. Work is its own reward. They don't need much. These were the lucky ones. Language fails to capture the horror of the unlucky ones.

You cannot become an UrRich, you had to be born one. They and only they had the freedom to follow a thought as far as they desired. Whatever they willed. And sometimes they willed conflict. Sometimes they desired merriment and the universe bent to their wills. Trillions wiped out in a moment if it would serve their needs in their constant game of oneupmanship, pursued at a lazy pace with each move casually unfolding over centuries. What Gods, what conception of reality as it were, could they not warp or turn to their purposes? Of what use magic? Science was but a metaphor. The Laws of Science themselves bowed to their every whim and fancy. How many billions of years had we toiled under them? Of that there is no record. No mention. It must have always been like this. The divinely ordained few who ruled with a carborundum fist. No word like dissent existed in the common vocabulary.

What would life be like then? To the average man or woman, for the most part. Not very different. You would do some bullshit menial back breaking work that had no meaning or significance. You toiled in silence for as long as you could take it. Then you died. Your remains were recycled meticulously. New life arose when old was extinguished. Every person toiling in their own private hell, following what command their mind told them to do. For as long as they can take it. The minds were not their own, the thoughts were not their own, even the body was not their own. It was a mishmash of genes hodgedpodged together from the rest of the refuse reclaimed a billion times over from those who had perished before. You emerged from the birthing vats, ready to follow orders and followed them till your bodies gave way. Then you were recycled and the New^TM You continued from where you stopped.
