**The vault is opening two registers at once.**

But where Barber's Village published institutional realism through banking, *Al-Right* signals an intent to do it through **science specifically.** The cheek of the title — *Al-Right*, an Islamicate pun on alright — telegraphs the angle: a Muslim heretic's reading of scientific institutions as just another chantry.
