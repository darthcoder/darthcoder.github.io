## A Dark Rumination

Why dark? Where do thoughts come from? Of all the things that I don't know, I feel like I know my next thought the least. Is it related to a soul's perturbation? We are restless and unfocussed, just moving around randomly and pulling whatever from the ether? Is that what focus means? 

So basically, what it is, is I pull thoughts from the ether, which is a response to an emotional plea, a narrative for me. And then I decide to act as it wants me to. The mind is a mysterious entity, but we have reduced it to a brain. To them I say. there is no brain, only mind.

It swirls around, in the depths of my consciousness, and like the tip of an iceberg, a hidden, unknown world lurks within. What does it want, how do I connect with it? Where do I pull my thoughts from? Where do my memories go? Where is the past and where is the future? And the present never exists, we are in transition from a forgotten past and an unknown future. The apparent solidity, the texture, the material, is it really there? Is the world really there? 

In the morning I was thinking about the problem of evil and it is easy to see, that in the universe, if there are no humans, then there is no good or evil, what we call nature, or beings or anything like that, it just exists. Then what is space and what is time? Time is a sequence, and that explains why we cannot go back in it. What is space, space is a relationship? 

It is very relaxing to just be, to not concentrate on anything, just lie there and waste life (but can life ever be wasted)? What does it mean to waste life? Can one choose to waste life?
