## Rediscovering purpose

I have always thought of life as a series of steps, each leading to something grander something more opulent, something more tangible that I can show around to others who have written me off and shout from the rooftops - here look, I matter. 

But, that has not panned out, much to my surprise and chagrin. And in hindsight, it was never going to work. All those hours all that torment and torture and I have nothing to show for. What hurts me the most is the realization that I will never be able to even change my job. That kills me from the inside. This is it. This is all I am worth. It is a really tough pill to swallow. Was that all that I have achieved and learnt? And what have I actually learnt? 10 years have gone by two and a half times. I am old, diseased and weak. I don't feel like I even have a divine spark left in me. I don't want to do anything. And time and time again, I find myself at these very same crossroads. There is nothing worth striving for. There is nothing to learn. Nothing to teach, no joy. Just a stasis to exist in. And of course, I can think of a million things to do, but the reward doesn't seem to justify the effort. Has it always been like that. In the end, I just hope for the destruction of the systems of the world, and maybe then, we will be free. 

I have never followed a thought to completion, never acted on my ideas, never pursued anything and already I have accepted defeat. What does it mean to achieve? What does it mean to finish? I just put things off for tomorrow. I just leave my mind open to the warp and lie there. Just lie there. I need to heal and I need to follow the straight path. I know that, but I feel guilty of trying to be a good person, because in essence I am a bad person, or at least I deserve to be punished, because I cannot be someone who deserves a good turn, a stroke of luck. And of course all the good things that happen to me don't matter and of course I am too burnt out to put in any effort at any task. What is the point of life? What am I even doing? When you do something, you lock yourself out of all the alternatives. So the best thing is to not do anything, that way all paths remain open forever. Your potential remains untapped and you could sing of your incoming glory, but there is nothing to do. Nothing that will soothe the heart. The mind races on and on on a circular track like a rodent, same old ideas, not even ideas, just the shape of ideas. This might be worth pursuing. Just download things. Can never start anything. Too many choices, too many things to do, too many ways to do things, no clear progression. How to get started? What to do? What about my intuition? I am lain to waste. I am forever tarnished. The flame flickers, barely.

And what does this achieve. The same whiny pointless drivel. The same word, I am irritated of expressing the same opinions over and over and over again. 

Is there even a God? Is He singling me out? Just shout in my head. I will hear and I will obey. Left adrift without purpose, forsaken. Lost, anonymous. This emotion, this pain, this open, festering wound that I have been carrying forever, that would not let me rest, that never seems to heal. That gnaws at my sinews. That sucks all the juices of nutrition. I can't relax. I can't heal. I can't turn my mind off. I can't acknowledge my blessings. The wound is too open. The pain too much to bear. I want to shut down. I want to relax. I want to just rest for a while. I am too tired to continue. I can't catch a break. I am always stuck. I was stuck in IITK, I was stuck in DPS, I was stuck in IITP, I was stuck in Hajipur and I am stuck here. Only momentary relief, but I don't remember what that used to feel like. I am tired. I can't seem to continue. I am exhausted. No one seems to understand. It would be better if everyone could see the burden I am carrying. Or perhaps it is better this way. God placed a burden the weight of a feather on me and already I complain. But I am tired. too tired to continue. Too exhausted. Just at my wits ends. Just everything feels pointless. I am overburdened. I have nothing interesting to say. No thoughts to communicate. No peace to share. No health to continue. I just keep moving on. I don't know why. Slowly I walk, crawl, stumble and I don't even know if I am moving forward or not. I just keep moving on. But I am stuck at the same place that I have always been nothing seems to be changing.
