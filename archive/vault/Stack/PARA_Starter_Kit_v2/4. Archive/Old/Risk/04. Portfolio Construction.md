Combines expected return and risk by a choice of investment. Higher risk is accompanied by higher return.
Sharpe ratio measures risk. ratio of avg. rate of return in excess of the risk free rate o the absolute risk.

**Asset allocation** is the process of constructing a portfolio across all risk classes.
**Correlation Coeff.** measures how two asset classes are related to each other.

## Sharpe Ratio

$$ S_i = \dfrac{\overline{(R_i)}- \overline{(RFR)}}{\overline{(\sigma_i)}} $$
where, $R_i$ = average return on portfolio $i$
$RFR$ = risk free return
$\sigma_i$ = the standard deviation of the rate of return of portfolio $i$
