# Self Hosting as a Service

The Cloud has a comparatively low barrier to entry and tremendous utility, most importantly you can scale up and down automatically. The promise is pay-per-use. However, the problem is - and there is no getting around it - Cloud Capitalism. You will almost never be scaling down. As such Cloud costs can balloon and grow prohibitive. Its however a source of tremendous complexity. Support is non-existent. Data is used to train AI models. If anything is offered for free then you are the product. Convenience has a price. All vendors have their own paradigms and its difficult to migrate, well nigh impossible to migrate. There is not a lot of documentation. 

## _Caveat Emptor:_ What you shouldn't self host

1. Mail
2. Payment systems - more like you can't self host.
