
How to apply mathematics to the real world?

Mathematics is a system of patterns. These patterns are connected together with extended rigor and formalism. However, this makes progress difficult and if the goal is to grok the ideas and the inter-relation of concepts then the proofs are not really necessary, especially if your goals are to apply mathematics to some phenomena or other discipline. In fact, it is my argument that insight is far more important.

What do we mean by an insight. 

> [!Definition] **Insight**
> To grok a concept and its inter-relations to other concepts.

To be honest we need a certain amount of formalism and rigor because it is necessary to be precise and natural language is fundamentally ambiguous, but we need to get rid of formalism for the sake of being formal so that our ideas could be disseminated to a wider audience. Of course any such endeavor will necessarily make the discourse imprecise and lack the standards of rigor established by centuries of peer review, but the sheer lack of mathematical understanding in not just the general population but the misapplication of mathematical  and statistical ideas by engineers, doctors, social scientists, psychologists and the like who should certainly know better and the swindling of the general populace by the media conglomerates, propagandists, politicians and their ilk all demand a better way of communicating mathematical ideas to the general public. 

On the one hand we can have the 3b1b  school of pretty pictures and animations, but even they have limited applicability as they are not being applied anywhere and it is just media for consumption in short form. Then we have platforms like brilliant which just weaponizes this.
